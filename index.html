A bit about me

Hello! I'm Fahad — a Full Stack MERN Developer based in Lahore, Pakistan. I love turning ambiguous product ideas into reliable, fast, and beautiful software. Over the last 3.5+ years I've delivered production-grade applications across fintech, CRM, HR, and AI-powered platforms.
